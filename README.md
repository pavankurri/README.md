# RetailNova Sales Analytics Project

## Overview
RetailNova Inc. is a mid-sized retail company that sells a wide range of consumer products including electronics, apparel, home & kitchen, and personal care items. The company operates through both online and offline channels, allowing customers to shop conveniently from anywhere.

This project analyzes RetailNova’s sales and return data to identify key business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product returns, and regional profitability.

---

## Business Problem
RetailNova experienced rapid growth in online orders during the pandemic. However, recent quarters have shown several challenges:

- Increasing product return rates
- Customer churn
- Declining profitability in certain regions and product categories

The objective of this project is to analyze the data and uncover insights that can help improve business performance and profitability.

---

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and revenue trends
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Measure and analyze product return rates
- Evaluate regional and store-level performance
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Data cleaning and EDA Analysis  
- **SQL Server (SSMS)** – Data extraction and querying  
- **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard creation  
- **DAX** – KPI and measure calculations  
- **Excel / CSV** – Data preparation and storage

---

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Revenue per Customer
- Year-over-Year Sales Growth
- Return Rate
- Top Selling Products
- Store Performance Metrics

---

## Key Insights
- A small group of products contributes significantly to total revenue.
- Certain categories show higher return rates affecting profitability.
- Some regions and stores are underperforming compared to others.
- Customer purchasing behavior varies across product categories.

---

## Recommendations
- Focus marketing and inventory planning on high-performing products.
- Investigate high return-rate products to reduce losses.
- Implement customer retention strategies to reduce churn.
- Improve sales strategies in low-performing regions.

---

## Project Outcome
This project demonstrates how data analytics and visualization can transform raw retail data into actionable insights that support better business decisions.
